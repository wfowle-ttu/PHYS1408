# 1 Dimensonal Motion:

Essential Equations

Solving for X final given X initial, velocity initial, velocity final, and time. \\ X_f = X_i + \frac{V_i+v_f}{2}\*t \\

\\ V_f = V_i + a\*t \\

\\ V_f^2 = V_i^2+2a(X_f-X_i) \\

\\ X_f = X_i+V_it+(1/2)\*a\*t^2 \\

For each of the above equations X can be substituted for Y and the same calculations can be used to find Vertical motion in 1D.
